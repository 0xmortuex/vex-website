Moving in takes about five minutes

Nothing to sign up for. Setup does the heavy lifting and every step can be skipped.

  1. Install

    Run the installer. There is no account to make.

  2. Bring your things over

    Setup offers your bookmarks and history from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Opera and Opera GX. Passwords come from the file you export from that browser; Vex never opens another browser's encrypted stores.

  3. Make it yours

    Choose a look, then drag the toolbar and the sidebar buttons where you want them. All of it can be changed later in Settings.

Websites will still ask you to sign in once: browsers keep their signed-in sessions to themselves. With your passwords imported, that is one click.

What is inside, by what you want to do

Hundreds of features, all in the browser. None of it is a paid add-on.

  • Tabs where you want themOn top like Chrome, down the side, or in a frosted Glass look. Groups, sleeping tabs that give their memory back, split screen with up to four pages, Picture-in-Picture.
  • One bar for everythingPress Ctrl+K to open a page, search, switch a panel or run any command, ranked by what you use.
  • Looks you can switchFirefox, Chrome, Safari, Glass and Classic, with themes, textures, fonts and a layout you can drag around.
  • Apps in the sidebarDiscord, WhatsApp, Spotify, GitHub and any site you pin, each in its own session with unread badges.
  • Dark mode that keeps photosTurns light websites dark from the first frame, leaves photos and logos alone, and steps aside when a site is already dark.
  • Viewers of its ownPDFs, pictures, JSON and CSV files and folders open inside Vex, with marks and forms on PDFs and a real file browser for folders.
  • Blocking that is on from the startAds and trackers, in the network and on the page, plus fingerprint protection, DNS over HTTPS and HTTPS-only mode.
  • Tor in one clickVex fetches and runs the official Tor bundle itself. Nothing else to install.
  • Past a blocked siteA built-in bypass for DNS and SNI blocking, with no VPN and no administrator rights.
  • Nothing phones homeNo analytics and no telemetry. A plain document lists every request Vex makes and why.
  • Say it in plain words“Close all the YouTube tabs.” “Group my GitHub tabs.” Common commands are understood on your computer, with no network.
  • Ask about what you are readingSummarize, explain or translate a page or a selection, and search everything you have read by meaning.
  • You choose where it runsIn the cloud on your own Cloudflare Worker, on your own machine with Ollama, or fully inside the browser. It stays off until you set it up.
  • It asks before it actsThe agent shows its plan, asks before anything it cannot undo, and Pause stops it between steps.
  • Streaming that playsNetflix, Spotify, Prime Video and Disney+ work, thanks to Widevine in the signed installer.
  • Screen share with real controlsPick the window, the resolution up to 1440p, the frame rate, and whether to share sound.
  • One volume for everythingA single slider from 0 to 500 percent across every tab, and a hub for what is playing.
  • Out of a game's wayWhen a full-screen game starts, Vex frees the graphics card and holds back its background work.
  • A player of its ownVideos and sounds open with subtitles, speed, A-B loop, cover art and a queue of the folder.
  • A toolbox of small toolsHundreds of calculators, converters and formatters, with the ones for your job at the top.
  • Notes, reminders, focusSticky notes on a page, reminders that wake your PC, and focus sessions that block distractions.
  • Sign-ins that just workAn encrypted password vault, two-factor codes that fill themselves in, and passkeys.
  • A Vex for each personProfiles with their own tabs, bookmarks, passwords and cookies, open side by side.
  • Documents without leavingWord, Excel, PowerPoint and e-book files open in a viewer instead of downloading, and Space previews any download.
  • Print the way you wantCtrl+P shows the pages as they will print, with paper, margins and pages per sheet.

    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. Vex is open source under the MIT licence, and nothing in it is a paid add-on.

    Does it run on Mac or Linux?

    Not yet. Vex is for Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

    Does Netflix work?

    Yes, in the signed installer from the Releases page. Widevine, which streaming sites need, is only in that build.

    Windows shows a warning when I run the installer.

    Vex is an independent app, so SmartScreen may not know it yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

    What does Vex send, and to whom?

    No analytics and no telemetry. Update checks, filter lists and anything you switch on, such as AI or sync, make requests; the privacy document lists each one.

    Do I need an AI account?

    No. The AI helper is optional and off until you connect your own Worker, a local Ollama model, or the on-device model.
